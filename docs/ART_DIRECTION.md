# Art & Myth Direction

Status: **Stage 0 locked**

## Form language

Use chunky, readable, low-poly geometry:

- faceted terrain
- terraced cliffs
- readable silhouettes
- stylized foliage
- clear height changes in a 3/4 camera

The low-poly form language is useful. A bright or playful palette is not.

## World palette

The world is desaturated, cold, hostile, and near-monochrome:

- deep slate blues
- bone whites
- wet browns
- dead greens
- heavy ambient darkness

Think **low-poly noir**.

## Magic palette

Magic is the primary saturated color in the frame and should appear emissive/blooming.

### Player magic

Cool spectrum:

- cyan
- violet
- white-hot cores

### Enemy magic

Rot spectrum:

- crimson
- bile green
- ember orange

### Loot rarity

Use a quieter third register:

- gold
- pale amber

Loot should not visually compete with active combat magic.

## UI reference rule

The WoW-style character sheet reference is for **information architecture**, not visual imitation:

- character silhouette centered
- gear slots readable around the character
- dense but legible stats
- strong equipment hierarchy

The final visual skin remains the game's own dark low-poly language.

## Biblical layer

Treat biblical material as **war-mythology**: mythic, respectful, not devotional and not satirical.

- **Nephilim / giants:** boss family.
- **Goliath:** mid-game wall; defeated through terrain intelligence, not pure damage racing.
- **Samson:** strength/endurance ruin-dungeon built around pillars and structural collapse.
- **David:** precision/ranged thematic line; sling as artifact inspiration.
- **Divinity remains off-screen.** God is not depicted as an NPC or boss. The world deals with aftermath, ruins, artifacts, giants, and consequences rather than direct divine exposition.
